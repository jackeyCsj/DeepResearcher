Threat models

Ø We need to describe the attacker's capabilities and skills:
v Naive model:
o No knowledge about the system, no access to large DBs o Access to RBRs + black-box system v Collision model: o Access to large DBs o Access to a BTP system that generates similar PIs to the system
under attack v General model:
o Full knowledge about the system, statistical properties of the enrolled data, access to protected data
o Standard model: secret key remains secure o Advanced model: chosen-plaintext and chosen-ciphertext attack o Full disclosure model: secret key compromised

Accuracy degradation

Ø Most BTP schemes transform either the sample (e.g. surface folding) or the template (e.g., fuzzy vault)
Ø That leads to the addition of noise or information loss, which in turn leads to a decrease in accuracy
Ø We need to assess such performance loss in accordance with the ISO/IEC 19795:
v Compute FMR and FNMR for the baseline system AND the BTP scheme v Following a common experimental protocol v Compare in terms of DET plots
o The Equal Error Rate (EER), where FMR = FNMR, is not enough!!

Irreversibility & Unlinkability

Ø Theoretical evaluation:
v "Proving a computational or an information-theoretic property limiting the success probability or the advantage of any computationallybounded adversary"
v Example for unlinkability: indistinguishability game describes the advantage of an attacker with respect to a perfect indistinguishable system

Ø Empirical evaluation:
v "The experimenter shall specify an empirical method of demonstrating irreversibility / unlinkability, for example, empirically estimating the success probability or the advantage over a random guess for some specific adversary in an attack scenario"

[ISO/IEC IS 30136 on Performance Testing of BTP Schemes]

Irreversibility analysis

Ø How can we analyse irreversibility? Following cryptographic paradigms?

Ø Careful! Some assumptions are not valid:
v Uniformity of data ­ neighbouring bits are correlated!! v In fact, some biometric templates (e.g., finger vein or fingerprint
minutiae spectral representation) are compared in terms of their cross correlation!
v There are also symmetries

Ø Therefore, we need to model such correlations and take them into account in the computations
v No general method proposed so far
